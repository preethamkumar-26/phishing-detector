Neural Architecture Blueprint

End-to-end 12-stage visualization of the multi-branch representation and custom residual network.

Stage 1

Candidate Email Ingestion & Parsing

Ingests MIME payloads, decodes quoted-printable/base64 strings, strips HTML noise, and splits subject, body, URLs, and MTA headers.

Stage 3–7

5-Branch Parallel Feature Extraction

Text Branch
768-d Contextual Projection
Financial Branch
18-d Specific Attributes
Security Branch
19-d Coercion Signals
URL & Metadata
31-d Structural Cues
Stage 8 & 9

Concatenation Fusion & Custom ResNet (From Scratch)

Combines all branches into an 836-d fused vector (h_fused). Passes through projection layer and dual residual blocks with layer normalization to produce a 256-d compact representation vector (h_repr) without relying on heavy BERT or CNN backbones.

Stage 10–12

Classification Head & Validation Thresholding

Evaluates P_phish ≥ T using calibrated thresholds (default 0.58) alongside three-pillar explainability audit logging.
